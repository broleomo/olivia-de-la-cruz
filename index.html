Bio

Raised in orchard country, built in the clubs of Seattle.

"Every song starts as a true story — I just find out how it ends while I'm writing it."

Born and raised in the valley of Lake Chelan, Washington, Olivia De La Cruz writes songs that sit at the crossroads of Americana, soul, and rock — sultry and lyrical, built from lived-in stories of travel, friendship, love, and heartbreak. Her self-titled debut arrived in 2013; her sophomore record, House of Memory, followed in 2019. Most recently, she returned to Seattle's legendary Robert Lang Studios to record the singles "Light" and "Muse and Composer."
